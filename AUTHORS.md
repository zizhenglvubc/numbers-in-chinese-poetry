## Origin and acknowledgements

The analysis was presented in July 2026 during an exchange program at Peking
University, as part of a group presentation on numerals in the Chinese language
(汉语的数字文化观). The group had four members, each of whom prepared an
independent section on a different body of material; the sections were combined
into a single deck for the final presentation.

This repository contains only the author's own section — numerals in poetry from
the Tang through the Qing — together with the corpus scan and the counting code,
which are the author's own work. The other three sections are not included and
are not covered by this release.

With thanks to the three fellow presenters, whose sections on classical fiction,
idioms and pre-Tang poetry shaped how this material came to be read.
