# Presentation

*有数之形，无数之意：隋唐至明清诗词中的数字文化观*
"Form that can be counted, meaning that cannot" — numerals in poetry from the
Sui-Tang period through the Ming and Qing. 15 slides, July 2026, Peking University.

The deck sets out four things numerals do in poetry: they give testimony
(Du Fu, *Shi hao li*), they set scale (Liu Zongyuan, *Jiang xue*), they organise
relation (Li Bai, *Yue xia du zhuo*), and they carry cultural memory through
allusion (Xin Qiji's 八百里, from the *Shishuo xinyu*). Slides 12 to 14 are a
source list of thirty numeral-bearing lines from the Tang, the Song, and the
Yuan-Ming-Qing period.
